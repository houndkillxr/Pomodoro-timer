
🍅 Auto-Flow Pomodoro Timer
The Auto-Flow Pomodoro Timer is a distraction-free, desktop-based productivity application built for students who need to maintain intense focus. By automating the transition between deep work sessions and restorative breaks, this tool ensures you stay in your "flow state" without needing to interact with the clock manually.

🎓 Why Students Need This
Many students struggle with "time blindness" or burnout. This tool is designed to solve these common academic challenges:

Eliminates Decision Fatigue: You don't have to worry about when to start your break or when to return to work. The app handles the transitions for you automatically, so you can keep your focus entirely on your textbooks, code, or essays.

Encourages Structured Deep Work: By using the Pomodoro technique, you break complex study blocks into manageable sprints. This prevents fatigue and keeps your mind sharp throughout long study sessions.

Visual Focus: The large, high-contrast interface and color-coded modes (Red for Work, Teal for Break) provide a clear visual cue of your status, even if you glance at it from across the room.

Customizable Pacing: Every student works differently. Our preset options allow you to choose your preferred intensity, from quick 25-minute sprints to long-form 60-minute deep-work sessions.
⚙️ How to Use
Automatic Start: The timer begins immediately upon launch, so you can start studying the moment you open the app.

Presets: Use the dropdown menu at the top to toggle between different work/break durations.

Flow Control: The app will automatically switch from your work session to your break (and back again) without requiring any clicks.

Manual Pause: If you need to step away unexpectedly, simply hit the PAUSE button. You can click RESUME whenever you are ready to get back into your groove.

🛠 Built With
Python: The core logic of the application.

QtPy: A powerful abstraction layer that allows the application to run smoothly across different Qt backends (PyQt6/PySide6).

Created to help students stay productive, hit their deadlines, and avoid burnout. Happy studying!
